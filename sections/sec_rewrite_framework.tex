\section{Encoding, joint symmetry, and cyclic accessibility}
\label{sec:rewrite-framework}

Let $A\in\{0,1\}^{n_B\times n_T}$ be the incidence matrix, with
$A_{bt}=1$ when base $b$ covers task $t$.  We first work in the physical
address space
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal H_{\rm valid}=(\mathbb C^{n_B})^{\otimes k};
 \label{eq:valid-space-rewrite}
\end{equation}
the embedding into $2^{\lceil\log_2n_B\rceil}$ levels and its dynamically
disconnected padding block are implementation details.

For a basis tuple $\boldsymbol b=(b_1,\ldots,b_k)$, let $U(\boldsymbol b)$
be the number of uncovered tasks and $D(\boldsymbol b)$ the number of equal
register pairs.  The problem Hamiltonian is
\begin{equation}
 H_{\rm prob}=H_{\rm walk}+\lambda H_{\rm cov}+\mu H_{\rm excl},
 \label{eq:hprob-rewrite}
\end{equation}
where the last two terms are diagonal with eigenvalues $U$ and $D$.  The
single-register complete-graph walk is normalized by $1/(n_B-1)$.  An
explicit initial Hamiltonian used for the linear interpolation is
\begin{equation}
 H_{\rm init}=\sum_{r=1}^k\bigl(I-\ket{u_B}\!\bra{u_B}\bigr)_r,
 \qquad
 \ket{u_B}=n_B^{-1/2}\sum_{b=1}^{n_B}\ket b,
 \label{eq:hinit-rewrite}
\end{equation}
with unique ground state $\ket u=\ket{u_B}^{\otimes k}$ and initial gap one.

\subsection{The faithfully represented group}

The colour-preserving incidence automorphism group is
\begin{equation}
 G_A=\{(\pi_B,\pi_T):A_{\pi_B(b),\pi_T(t)}=A_{bt}\}.
\end{equation}
Only its base component acts on Eq.~\eqref{eq:valid-space-rewrite}.  We
therefore use the effective group
\begin{equation}
 G_B=\operatorname{pr}_B(G_A)\simeq G_A/\ker\rho,
 \qquad \rho(\pi_B,\pi_T)=\pi_B,
\end{equation}
rather than treating a possibly non-faithful $G_A$ representation as if all
its irreps could occur.  The register and base actions commute, so the
represented symmetry is
\begin{equation}
 G=S_k\times G_B.
\end{equation}
Every term in Eqs.~\eqref{eq:hprob-rewrite} and \eqref{eq:hinit-rewrite}
commutes with $G$.  Consequently
\begin{align}
 \mathcal H_{\rm valid}
 &\simeq\bigoplus_{\alpha,\beta}
 V_\alpha^{S_k}\otimes V_\beta^{G_B}
 \otimes\mathcal M_{\alpha\beta},\\
 H&\simeq\bigoplus_{\alpha,\beta}
 I_{V_\alpha}\otimes I_{V_\beta}
 \otimes h_{\alpha\beta}.
 \label{eq:commutant-rewrite}
\end{align}
The second identity is the source of Schur multiplicities; an equality of
eigenvalues belonging to different blocks is a separate phenomenon.

\subsection{Allowed space versus accessible space}

The joint fixed projector and its range are
\begin{align}
 \Pi_{\rm sym}
 &=\left(\frac1{k!}\sum_{\sigma\in S_k}U_\sigma\right)
 \left(\frac1{|G_B|}\sum_{g\in G_B}V_g\right),\\
 \mathcal H_{\rm sym}&=\Ran\Pi_{\rm sym}.
 \label{eq:hsym-rewrite}
\end{align}
This is the symmetry-allowed invariant space, not automatically the space
filled by the protocol.  Let
$\mathfrak A=\operatorname{alg}^*(H_{\rm init},H_{\rm prob},I)$ be the unital
star-algebra generated by the endpoints.  The dynamically accessible
cyclic space is
\begin{align}
 \mathcal K_u&=\mathfrak A\ket u\\
 &=\operatorname{span}\{A_m\cdots A_1\ket u:\
 A_i\in\{H_{\rm init},H_{\rm prob}\}\}.
 \label{eq:ku-rewrite}
\end{align}
It is the smallest common reducing subspace that contains the initial state,
and in general
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal K_u\subseteq\mathcal H_{\rm sym}
 \subseteq\mathcal H_{\rm valid}.
 \label{eq:nested-spaces-rewrite}
\end{equation}
The first inclusion can be strict; padding makes it strict automatically if
one defines the symmetry on the full qubit space.

\subsection{Exact orbit quotient}

Orbit quotients of symmetry-confined quantum walks are established
constructions~\cite{KroviBrun2007}; we use the joint action here to retain
the exact Hamiltonian matrix elements relevant to the present encoding.
For a $G$-orbit $\mathcal O$ in the computational basis, define
$\ket{\mathcal O}=|\mathcal O|^{-1/2}\sum_{x\in\mathcal O}\ket x$.
These orbit states are an orthonormal basis of $\mathcal H_{\rm sym}$.  If
$H$ is invariant and $x\in\mathcal O$, then
\begin{equation}
 \langle\mathcal O'|H|\mathcal O\rangle
 =\sqrt{\frac{|\mathcal O|}{|\mathcal O'|}}
 \sum_{y\in\mathcal O'}H_{yx}.
 \label{eq:orbit-quotient-rewrite}
\end{equation}
The sum is independent of the representative.  Burnside's lemma gives
\begin{equation}
 \dim\mathcal H_{\rm sym}
 =\frac1{|G_B|}\sum_{g\in G_B}[z^k]
 \prod_{\ell\geq1}(1-z^\ell)^{-c_\ell(g)},
 \label{eq:burnside-rewrite}
\end{equation}
where $c_\ell(g)$ counts length-$\ell$ cycles in the base action.  Orbit
states need not span $\mathcal K_u$ minimally; cyclic closure must be tested
separately.
