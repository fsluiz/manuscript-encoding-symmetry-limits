\section{Sector-resolved localization}
\label{sec:rewrite-localization}

Let $P$ project onto the span of computational-basis tuples that use
distinct bases and cover every task, and put $Q=I-P$.  Write
$V=\lambda H_{\rm cov}+\mu H_{\rm excl}$,
$m=\min\{\lambda,\mu\}$, and $\kappa=k/(n_B-1)$.  Then $VP=0$ and the
complete-graph normalization gives
\begin{equation}
 QH_{\rm prob}Q\geq(m-\kappa)Q.
 \label{eq:q-lower-rewrite}
\end{equation}

The global localization statement cannot be copied verbatim into an
arbitrary sector.  A projected cover is a superposition of computational
covers and can have nonzero kinetic expectation.  The missing term is the
sector's kinetic floor.

\begin{proposition}[Sector localization]
\label{prop:sector-localization-rewrite}
Let $\Pi_\gamma$ be a symmetry-sector projector commuting with
$H_{\rm prob}$, $H_{\rm walk}$, $V$, and $P$, and suppose
$P_\gamma=P\Pi_\gamma\ne0$.  Define
\begin{align}
 Q_\gamma&=Q\Pi_\gamma,&
 B_\gamma&=Q_\gamma H_{\rm walk}P_\gamma,\\
 \eta_\gamma&=\lambda_{\min}\!\left(
 P_\gamma H_{\rm walk}P_\gamma|_{\Ran P_\gamma}\right).
\end{align}
If $Q_\gamma=0$, every state in the sector lies in $\Ran P$ and the leakage
is identically zero.  Otherwise, let $E_0^\gamma$ be the lowest energy in the
sector and define the compressed invalid-state separation
\begin{equation}
 \Gamma_\gamma=\lambda_{\min}\!\left(
 Q_\gamma H_{\rm prob}Q_\gamma|_{\Ran Q_\gamma}\right)-E_0^\gamma.
\end{equation}
If $\Gamma_\gamma>0$, every normalized sector ground state $\psi_\gamma$
satisfies
\begin{equation}
 1-\langle\psi_\gamma|P|\psi_\gamma\rangle
 \leq\frac{\|B_\gamma\|^2}
 {\Gamma_\gamma^2+\|B_\gamma\|^2}.
 \label{eq:sector-bound-rewrite}
\end{equation}
Moreover,
\begin{equation}
 \Gamma_\gamma\geq m-\kappa-\eta_\gamma,
 \label{eq:sector-separation-rewrite}
\end{equation}
so the right-hand side of Eq.~\eqref{eq:sector-bound-rewrite} remains valid
with $\Gamma_\gamma$ replaced by
$g_\gamma=m-\kappa-\eta_\gamma>0$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The variational principle applied inside $\Ran\Pi_\gamma$ gives
$E_0^\gamma\leq\eta_\gamma$.  Combining this with
Eq.~\eqref{eq:q-lower-rewrite} proves
Eq.~\eqref{eq:sector-separation-rewrite}.  For a sector ground vector, write
$p=P_\gamma\psi_\gamma$ and $q=Q_\gamma\psi_\gamma$.  The projected
eigenvalue equation is
\begin{equation}
 (Q_\gamma H_{\rm prob}Q_\gamma-E_0^\gamma)q=-B_\gamma p.
\end{equation}
The inverse on the left has norm at most $\Gamma_\gamma^{-1}$, hence
$\|q\|\leq\|B_\gamma\|\|p\|/\Gamma_\gamma$.  Using
$\|p\|^2+\|q\|^2=1$ gives Eq.~\eqref{eq:sector-bound-rewrite}.
\end{proof}

This statement certifies the solution content of the lowest-energy state
within a sector.  It does not say that the ground vector has exact support on
$\Ran P$: for finite penalties Eq.~\eqref{eq:sector-bound-rewrite} controls,
rather than eliminates, leakage.  Exact support occurs only in an infinite
penalty limit or in special decoupled cases.

There is also a cyclic-space caveat.  The projector onto $\mathcal K_u$ need
not commute with $P$, so $P\Pi_{\mathcal K}$ need not be an orthogonal
projector.  Proposition~\ref{prop:sector-localization-rewrite} must be used
for genuine symmetry sectors; cyclic-space solution probability is then
evaluated directly or bounded through a separately constructed commuting
projector.
